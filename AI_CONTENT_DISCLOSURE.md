# AI content disclosure for Steam

## Proposed Steam Content Survey response

Select **Pre-Generated AI**.

Suggested disclosure text:

> Generative AI tools were used during development to create the game's
> pre-generated 2D visual assets. These assets were selected, edited, cropped,
> color-corrected, animated, integrated, and quality-controlled by the
> developer. The shipped game does not generate visual, audio, text, or other
> content with AI while it is running, and it does not accept player prompts.

Adjust that text if any runtime AI is added before release.
